What is the Mishneh Torah?

The Mishneh Torah (משנה תורה) is the Rambam's complete code of Jewish law, written around 1180.

It covers all of halacha in clear Hebrew, arranged in 14 books, 83 topics and all 613 commandments.

This app takes you through it step by step.

Why learn it?

Gedolei Yisroel throughout the generations have emphasized the importance of knowing all 613 mitzvos, as this deepens our understanding of Hashem’s will and our connection to His Torah. Some of their inspiring words are presented below.

To serve the King, know His commands. You can't be a loyal servant without knowing what He asks. (Kitzur Chareidim)

Learning them counts as doing them. Learning all 613 is counted as though you fulfilled them. (Smak, R' Chaim Vital)

Know them to live them. Like a merchant who must know what he came to buy, you need to know the mitzvos to catch every chance to do one. (Chafetz Chaim)

"Learn the Rambam's Mishneh Torah in order to know the 613 mitzvos." (Ohr HaYashar)

Happy is the one who arrives with his learning in his hand. (Shulchan Aruch HaRav)

The Rambam's Phone Number

Each digit is how many hilchot are in one sefer, in order.

  • 5-6-10 = Madda 5, Ahavah 6, Zemanim 10
  • 5-3-4 = Nashim, Kedushah, Haflaah
  • 7-9-6 = Zeraim, Avodah, Korbanot
  • 8-5-5-5-5 = Taharah, Nezikin, Kinyan, Mishpatim, Shoftim

Dial it once and you know all 14 sefarim and all 83 hilchot.

Why learn the 613: 29 sources

  1. Sefer Chareidim & Divrei Chayim: "I want to be a good soldier." A soldier must know the King's orders.
  2. Mesilas Yesharim: If I really love my beloved, I will search out every nuance that makes him happy. Show your love for Hashem.
  3. Sefer Chareidim: How else can I do teshuva if I don't know all the prohibitions?
  4. Mesilas Yesharim: I will not lose the countless opportunities to earn Olam Haba.
  5. Rambam, Hilchos Lulav: Being happy and in love with Hashem.
  6. Rambam, Hilchos Temura: The mitzvos have the power to help me overcome the Yetzer Hara and refine my character.
  7. Sefer HaMitzvos HaKatzer: Mitzvos d'Oraisa need kavana, so one has to know them all to have kavana for them.
  8. Bamidbar 15:39, Tzitzis: Predicated on knowing the 613 mitzvos in order to do them.
  9. Sefer Chareidim: Recite the Taryag Mitzvos every day or week, to be aware of every opportunity, and learn them by heart.
  10. Sefer Chareidim: Start the day with a verbal reading of all the Taryag Mitzvos, then recite them from memory.
  11. The Smak: Reciting the Taryag Mitzvos counts as having done them. Learn two or three mitzvos a day in depth.
  12. R' Chaim Vital: A Jew has to fulfil all 613 in thought, words and action. Reciting all 613 fulfils all three.
  13. Ohr HaYashar: Learn the Rambam's Mishneh Torah or his Sefer HaMitzvos in order to know the Taryag Mitzvos in depth too.
  14. The Shach, Poel Tzedek: Reciting the Taryag Mitzvos each day fulfils "this Torah shall not depart from your mouth."
  15. Rabbeinu Yitzchak Katz: Reciting all 613 counts as though one did them, saving oneself from a gilgul.
  16. The Chida: Follow the Shach: recite all the Taryag Mitzvos at least once a week. It counts as though he did them all.
  17. The Radvaz: Carry a pocket list of the Taryag Mitzvos wherever you go and review them daily.
  18. Shelah HaKadosh: Anyone who loves Hashem should commit the Taryag Mitzvos to memory.
  19. Shelah HaKadosh: Learn the Taryag Mitzvos first, because learning them brings one to do them and become a yerei chet.
  20. Sefer Derech Chaim: Learn 100 mitzvos a day and 13 on Shabbos, completing all 613 once a week.
  21. Shulchan Aruch HaRav: Happy is the one who comes here with his learning in his hand. "His learning" refers to the Taryag Mitzvos.
  22. Ohr HaShanim: Every melamed should teach his students all the Taryag Mitzvos in the pesukim with Rashi, or at least those that apply today.
  23. R' Moshe Leib of Sassov: First master the Taryag Mitzvos thoroughly, only then learn pilpul.
  24. R' Moshe Chassid of Prague: Once you know the Taryag Mitzvos by heart, you can review them anytime. Review three a day and they will stay on the tip of your tongue.
  25. Rabbi Greenwald of Poppa: To remember the Taryag Mitzvos, find a new insight of your own in each one.
  26. Chafetz Chaim, Shmiras HaLashon: Without knowing the Taryag Mitzvos we can't fully fulfil tzitzis. Like a merchant who must first know what he came to buy.
  27. R' Mottel Slonimer: Teach children all the Taryag Mitzvos by heart: "place it in their mouths."
  28. Harav from Petisberg, Hadras Melech: Review the mitzvos every month until you know them by heart. Count them on your fingers.
  29. Rav Yosef Chaim Zonnenfeld: Taught his son the Taryag Mitzvos instead of a Bar Mitzvah drasha: how can he accept the yoke of mitzvos without knowing them?
